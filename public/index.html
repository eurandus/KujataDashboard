Kujata flip board

Find FFXIV crafting materials worth reselling on Kujata, using live Market Board data.

What this site is for

In Final Fantasy XIV, many players pay extra on the Market Board to skip a teleport, a gathering trip or a crafting step. This board finds the crafting materials, ingredients and consumables where you can profit from that convenience on Kujata.

It buys nothing and sells nothing. For each item you list, it checks current Market Board data from Universalis and tells you what is worth buying, from where, and how much you could earn.

It helps you answer: What sells fast on Kujata? What can I buy cheaply from an NPC? After the Market Board tax, what do I actually keep? Where should my gil go first?

How to use it

  1. Check the item list on the left. Edit it, or add your own items, one per line.
  2. Set your share (the part of Kujata's daily sales you expect to win), the tax, and your budget.
  3. Press Scan, then read the three picks and the table. Click any column heading to sort, or click an item name to see its last 10 sales.
  4. Before buying, confirm the NPC vendor and location on Garland Tools (garlandtools.org), then list a small test batch in game.
What the table columns mean
NPC cost
Gil you pay per unit. Enter it yourself if you know it.
Lowest listing
The cheapest normal-quality listing on Kujata right now. This is what you compete against.
7d median
The middle sale price from the past week. One strange sale cannot move it much.
List at
Suggested price: 1% under today's cheapest listing, at least 1 gil. It uses current listings, not past averages. A "!" means it is below break-even or there are no competitors.
Sold in 7d
Units that actually sold on Kujata in the past week. An item with 0 is never picked.
Sold/day
Units sold per day on average.
Listings
How many sellers you compete with. Fewer is better.
Net/unit
Price after tax, minus cost. The price used is the lower of "List at" and the 7d median, so it is cautious.
Daily profit
Net/unit × the units per day you can realistically win. That is the lower of your share and a fair split among all current sellers. Table ranking uses this.
Start qty
A cautious first purchase: about 3 days of your realizable sales, within your budget. Items at 0 are excluded from picks.
Data
Fresh means a player uploaded Kujata data in the last 48 hours. Stale is 2–7 days, Very stale is over 7 days.
Flags
Warnings such as a market price 10x or more above the NPC price (check the NPC price is right), high one-off sales, or inflated listings. Click the item name to read them.
Good to know
Prices
They come from players' uploads, so quiet items can be stale or missing. Check the fetched time under the table.
Asterisk (*)
The NPC price is from game data, and no vendor is confirmed. Verify it first.
Best habit
Many small items that sell daily beat one item that might sell next month.
